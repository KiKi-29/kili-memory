## About the Student Engagement App

### What It Is
A **two-sided platform**:
- **Mobile App** — Student-facing. Helps students navigate their entire college academic journey from enrollment through graduation.
- **Web App** — Advisor-facing. Built natively on **Salesforce Education Cloud**. Gives advisors a clear, real-time view of individual students and the full cohort.

### Why It's a Strong Fit for This Audience

#### 1. Native Salesforce (Web App)
The advisor web app is **built on Education Cloud** — not integrated with Salesforce, but actually living inside it. This means:
- No security review for a new external tool
- No data leaving Salesforce
- No new vendor onboarding
- No additional IT infrastructure
- Advisors work inside the platform they already know
- Faster time to value

#### 2. SIS Integration (Mobile App)
The mobile app integrates with both **Salesforce and major SIS platforms** (Banner, PeopleSoft, Workday, etc.) — connecting the student experience to the systems of record these universities already run.

#### 3. Solves a Known Pain Point
These personas are actively struggling with:
- Data siloed across SIS + Salesforce + LMS
- Advisors switching between multiple tools to get a full student picture
- Students not knowing where to go or who to talk to
- Lack of early intervention signals

The Student Engagement App is the **connective tissue** that unifies these systems into one student experience and one advisor view.
