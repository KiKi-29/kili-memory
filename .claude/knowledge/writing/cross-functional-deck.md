# The Cross-Functional deck, and what does not go in it

Kiki presents the Deep Dive when the cycle is Sales and Marketing. The room is Satha, Doug,
Jennifer, Michael, Kathryn, Prabitha, Neethi, Sunil, Hema, Sridhar, Lina. It is the most
senior room she stands in.

---

## Her framework, in her own words

1. Read the previous Sales and Marketing decks for this meeting.
2. From the last cycle where Sales and Marketing presented to this one, list everything that
   happened.
3. Group as **wins, learnings, new initiatives, activities**.
4. Look forward six months. What will be done becomes the key area of focus.
5. The spine is the contrast: **what we have been optimising so far, and what we are going to
   be optimising next.**
6. **Then** data, to support, validate, and rule things out.

Narrative first, data last. Data does not generate the story, it tests it.

---

## Three things she cut, 2026-08-19

She was handed three findings and cut all three with "No need". Recorded exactly, because the
pattern is more useful than the instances:

1. **The Google Ads security incident and suspension.** Compromised early July via a vendor
   employee's malware, two rogue MCC links, account suspended 30 July, still suspended, CEO
   already escalating, two open Compromised Site violations.
2. **That no deliberate paid activity happened in the window.** Every campaign state change on
   record was involuntary. Quora in billing limbo since April, Reddit invitation never
   accepted, LinkedIn segment firing with no campaign action.
3. **The gaps left in the SEO programme after Sakshi's departure.** Weekly organic report
   stopped at Week 27, GSC and GA4 access stalled, Reddit and Quora handed over with no
   publishing since, the 5 May audit report and its cadence never delivered.

**She gave the instruction, not the reason.** What follows is inference and is marked as such
rather than dressed up as her rule.

What the three share: each is an operational problem that is either already owned and
escalated somewhere else, or attributes a shortfall to a person or a vendor. None of them is
a win, a learning she intends to act on, a new initiative, or an activity. They do not fit any
of her four buckets, and a Deep Dive is thirty minutes of argument rather than a status
report on what is broken.

**So the working filter, until she says otherwise:** before an item goes in, ask which of her
four buckets it belongs to. If the honest answer is "none, it is a problem", leave it out.
Being already escalated elsewhere is a reason to leave it out, not a reason to repeat it.

---

## What being cut from the deck does not mean

**Cut from the deck is not cut from her briefing.** She still has to be able to answer if
somebody in that room asks. So a cut item stays available to her as a prepared answer, and it
never gets deleted from the underlying inventory.

Keep the full inventory intact and separate from the deck material. The deck is a selection
from it, not a replacement for it.

---

## Provenance in this room

Every figure carries its source. She is standing in front of the CEO, and a number she cannot
defend when asked costs her more than a thinner deck would. If a claim does not survive its
own numbers, cut the claim and record what was cut and why. **The ruled-out items are a
deliverable**, not a failure, because she asked the data step to rule things out.

---

## She cuts hard, and simple is the target

2026-08-19. Handed a full grouped inventory across four buckets, she cut it to almost
nothing and said: **"We will keep it short, sweet and extremley simple like that. No need to
throw in any other stuff for this one."**

What survived out of a very large inventory:

- Three field events executed end to end. NASPA SSHE Austin as Silver Sponsor with Prabitha's
  Learning Lab and five booth meetings booked, NAEH Washington DC, IDA Toronto locked 1 to 3
  September with the podcast studio booked.
- **Agent 0 implementation only.** Her words: "Talk about Agent 0 implementation. That is all
  I can confirm." The rest of the agent estate, the Blog Publisher MCP, Claude on Teams, the
  voice skill, did not survive that sentence. **"That is all I can confirm" is a provenance
  standard, not a preference.** She will not stand behind something in that room she cannot
  personally vouch for, and neither should you propose it.
- Three focus areas for the next six months, and no more: a demand generation team running
  webinars, podcasts and roundtables as a content engine; Cities and Counties as market
  expansion; Custom App Development as market expansion.

**Cut because it was already reported:** the District360 website. Her words: "we called it out
already." An item that appeared in a previous cycle's deck does not reappear in this one, even
when it was a genuine win. Check the prior decks before proposing anything.

**The habit to build:** propose less than you think. She will ask for more if she wants it,
and she has never once asked for more. Every round with her has been a subtraction. A Deep
Dive that holds four ideas she can defend beats one that holds twenty she has to caveat.
